Process

Three steps to a great event

Meetakk handles the logistics so you can focus on the conversation.

  1. 1

    Join with your invite link

    Event organizers send participants a personal invite link. Click it or enter your token to authenticate directly in the browser — no account required.

  2. 2

    Meet your matched partner

    When the round starts, Meetakk pairs you with a compatible partner based on your selected interests. Your live video session begins automatically.

  3. 3

    Continue through every round

    After each timed round, the platform advances automatically to your next pairing. You stay on the same page — no refreshing, no manual steps.

Capabilities

Built for structured networking

Every feature is designed around the rhythm of a real speed-meeting event.

Structured multi-round sessions

Events run in sequential timed rounds. Each round is scheduled and orchestrated server-side so all participants stay perfectly in sync without manual coordination.

Interest-based partner matching

Participants select interest tags at registration. The matching engine uses those tags to pair people with complementary or shared interests across every round.

Browser-based live video

Video runs entirely in the browser via WebRTC — no app installation needed. Both direct peer connections and relay (TURN) paths are supported for reliable connectivity.

Automatic reconnection recovery

Temporary drops in connectivity are handled gracefully. The client reconnects to the WebSocket and resumes ICE negotiation within a configurable grace window.

Product

The call room keeps you focused on the person, not the platform.

During a round you see your partner's live video, a countdown timer, their name and interest tags, and a round-progress indicator. When the round ends, the platform moves you forward automatically.
